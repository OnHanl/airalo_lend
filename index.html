Frequently asked questions

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card built into your phone. Instead of inserting a physical card, you install a data plan electronically — usually by scanning a QR code — and it works alongside or instead of your regular SIM.

Most phones released since 2018, including recent iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, and Google Pixel models, support eSIM. We recommend checking your device settings for "Add eSIM" or "Cellular Plan" before you travel.
